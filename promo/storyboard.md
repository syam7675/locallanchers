## Production

The edit follows the actual generated voice durations in `motion/timing.json`. Motion uses masks, photographic parallax, typography reveals, perspective transforms and motivated wipes. Voice is mixed in front of an original, ducked instrumental bed. A restrained fade ends the film.

## Actual tools and authorization

Figma and ElevenLabs were requested but were unavailable. After the user instructed proceeding with whatever was required, the replacement was produced with editable HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Chromium, FFmpeg and Kokoro ONNX neural narration (af_heart). No Figma or ElevenLabs output is claimed. The earlier rejected video was removed from the current branch.

## Authenticity

Original logo, photographs and rendered website interfaces come from https://locallanchers.vercel.app. Industry imagery is illustrative, not a customer endorsement. Cards labeled Visibility, Conversion and Retention are editorial motion graphics, not invented application screens. Website team profiles and testimonials labeled as placeholders are excluded. No guaranteed outcomes or invented metrics appear.
